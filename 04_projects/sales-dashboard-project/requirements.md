# Project Requirements

A PDF version of this document is in [Project_Requirements.pdf](Project_Requirements.pdf).

Build two dashboards in Tableau that help the stakeholders, the sales managers and the executives, analyze the sales performance and the customers.

## Sales Dashboard

### Purpose
Present an overview of the sales metrics and trends, to analyze the year-over-year sales performance and understand the sales trends.

### Key Requirements
- **KPI Overview**: Display a summary of the total sales, profit and quantity for the current year and the previous year.
- **Sales Trends**: Present the data of each KPI by month, for the current year and the previous year. Make the months with the highest and the lowest sales easy to recognize.
- **Product Subcategory Comparison**: Compare the sales of the product subcategories in the current year and the previous year, and compare the sales with the profit.
- **Weekly Trends for Sales & Profit**: Present the weekly sales and profit of the current year with the average weekly values. Highlight the weeks above and below the average.

---

## Customer Dashboard

### Purpose
Provide an overview of the customer data, trends and behaviors, so the marketing teams and the management understand the customer segments and improve customer satisfaction.

### Key Requirements
- **KPI Overview**: Display a summary of the total number of customers, the total sales per customer and the total number of orders for the current year and the previous year.
- **Customer Trends**: Present the data of each KPI by month, for the current year and the previous year. Make the months with the highest and the lowest sales easy to recognize.
- **Customer Distribution by Number of Orders**: Show how the customers are distributed by the number of orders they placed, for insights into customer behavior, loyalty and engagement.
- **Top 10 Customers by Profit**: Present the 10 customers who generated the highest profit, with their rank, number of orders, current sales, current profit and last order date.

---

## Design & Interactivity (both dashboards)

### Dashboard Dynamic
- Let the users check the historical data by selecting any year.
- Let the users move between the two dashboards easily.
- Make the charts interactive, so the users can filter the data by clicking on a chart.

### Data Filters
- **Product**: category and subcategory.
- **Location**: region, state and city.
